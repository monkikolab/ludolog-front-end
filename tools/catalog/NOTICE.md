# Ludolog game catalog

The data in this catalog is compiled from four free sources and is distributed under the
**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0** licence (CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

## Sources

- **libretro-database** — https://github.com/libretro/libretro-database — CC BY-SA 4.0.
  Game identity (the No-Intro, Redump, FinalBurn Neo and MAME names, checksums and serials) and
  the genre, developer, publisher, release date and franchise fields. © the libretro-database
  contributors.
- **Wikidata** — https://www.wikidata.org — CC0 1.0. Genres, first release date, developers,
  publishers, series, and the number of Wikipedia articles about each game.
- **Wikipedia** — https://en.wikipedia.org — CC BY-SA 4.0. Each synopsis is the first paragraph
  of the game's English Wikipedia article, shortened. The title of the article it comes from is
  given with every synopsis, and the article is at `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<title>`.
  © the Wikipedia contributors.
- **GameTDB** — https://www.gametdb.com — used as its FAQ allows for software, with this link
  and notice. Genres, release dates, developers and publishers of Nintendo GameCube, Wii, Wii U,
  DS, 3DS, Switch and PlayStation 3 games, and the English synopsis of the games Wikipedia has no
  article for (marked `gt` in the synopsis files). © the GameTDB contributors.

## Changes

The sources were filtered to the fields listed above, merged by checksum, serial and title, and
the synopses were shortened. Nothing else was added.
